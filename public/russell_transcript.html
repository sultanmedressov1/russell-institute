Reviewer Feedback
This essay stands out for its strong attempt to analyze economic inequality not only as a moral issue, but as a structural feature of capitalism that can both stimulate innovation and undermine social mobility. One of its greatest strengths is the balanced approach, acknowledging the productive role of inequality while critically examining monopolization, technological polarization, and declining competition. The discussion of Google, AI-driven labor shifts, and the erosion of the middle class adds contemporary relevance and analytical depth to the argument. Particularly impressive is the policy-oriented dimension of the essay, including the proposed ISA framework, differentiated educational capital, and integration of stakeholder capitalism into Kazakhstan’s economic context.
